DATA DICTIONARY

All source data are downloaded by analysis.py from the World Bank Open Data API.

processed/world_income_health_panel.csv
- iso3: ISO3 economy code
- year: calendar year, 1990–2023
- gdp_per_capita: GDP per capita at PPP in constant 2021 international dollars
- life_expectancy: life expectancy at birth in years
- population: total population
- iso2: ISO2 economy code
- country: economy name
- region: World Bank region
- income_group: World Bank income classification

processed/regional_weighted_trends.csv
- year and region identify each observation
- countries is the number of complete economies
- population is the summed population represented
- life_expectancy is a population-weighted mean
- gdp_per_capita is a population-weighted mean

processed/annual_summary.csv
- annual global sample size, population-weighted outcomes, correlation, and the OLS slope measuring life-years associated with a doubling of GDP per capita
