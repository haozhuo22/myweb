# Data dictionary

All records are synthetic and generated by `analysis.py` with random seed 6400.

## raw/people.csv

- `person_id`: unique individual identifier
- `family_id`: shared identifier used to link one parent and one child
- `role`: parent or child
- `birth_year`: simulated year of birth

## raw/income_panel.csv

- `person_id`: individual identifier linked to people.csv
- `year`: calendar year of the simulated observation
- `age`: age at the observation
- `income`: simulated annual income in constant dollars

## processed/matched_parent_child.csv

One row per family. It retains family_id, parent_id, and child_id, plus parent income at age 45, child income at ages 25 and 35, and multi-year averages for both generations.
