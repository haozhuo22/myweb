All files are simulated by analysis.py; no external data are used.

bootstrap_sample.csv
  value: one observation from the Gamma(shape=4, scale=0.25) population.

bootstrap_distribution.csv
  bootstrap_mean: mean from one with-replacement resample of the original sample.

coverage_results.csv
  sample_size: observations in each simulated sample.
  confidence_level: nominal percentile-bootstrap confidence level.
  empirical_coverage: share of repeated intervals containing the true mean (1.0).
  experiments: repeated samples used to estimate coverage.
  bootstrap_resamples: resamples used for each interval.

null_distribution.csv
  null_difference_in_means: simulated difference between two sample means when H0 is true.

hypothesis_test_results.csv
  observed_difference: selected test statistic.
  p_value: two-sided simulation p-value.
  critical_absolute_difference: simulated 5% two-sided rejection cutoff.
  reject_at_5_percent: test decision.
